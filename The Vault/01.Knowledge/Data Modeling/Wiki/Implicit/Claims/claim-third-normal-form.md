# 3NF Eliminates Update Anomalies

<!-- A table in Third Normal Form avoids insertion, update, and deletion anomalies because every non-key field depends only on the primary key. Changing a department manager requires one update, not N employee rows. -->

## Claim

A table in Third Normal Form avoids insertion, update, and deletion anomalies because every non-key field depends only on the primary key. Changing a department manager requires one update, not N employee rows.
