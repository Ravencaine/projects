# Abbreviation Explanations

Every abbreviation used on a dashboard must be explained — either inline in the title/label, or in a dedicated legend or footnote.

## Principle

Domain-specific acronyms are one of the fastest ways to alienate or confuse a dashboard audience. Terms like ETA, MoM, YoY, SKU, and COGS are second nature to the people who use them daily, but acronyms introduced from other domains or new team members will not decode them without help. The reader should never have to guess what a value represents.

## Guidelines

- Never assume universal understanding of any abbreviation
- Define each abbreviation at first use in the title or label (e.g., "Gross Margin % (GM%)")
- If abbreviations are used repeatedly, add a glossary page or footnote section
- New team members or cross-functional audiences are the benchmark: if they cannot read it, the abbreviation is not acceptable without explanation
- Note: abbreviation explanations are especially critical in operational and finance dashboards where domain jargon is dense

## Examples

| Abbreviation | Full Term |
|-------------|-----------|
| ETA | Estimated Time of Arrival |
| MoM | Month over Month |
| YoY | Year over Year |
| SKU | Stock Keeping Unit |
| COGS | Cost of Goods Sold |
| DSO | Days Sales Outstanding |
| OEE | Overall Equipment Effectiveness |
