# Report Structural Integrity

Structural integrity is the foundation that makes every other report design principle work — a coherent structure enables clarity, guides the reader, and reduces cognitive load before a single chart is evaluated.

## Definition

Structural integrity in Power BI reports means the report has a deliberate, consistent physical organisation: a clear opening on each page, logical content sequencing, and uniform visual formatting throughout. It is the architectural discipline of report layout.

## Key Points

- **Lucid introduction:** every page should open with a clear statement of what question it answers; titles and page headers serve as the opening sentence
- **Logical content sequencing:** sections follow the reader's natural decision process: overview → trend → exception → detail
- **Uniform formatting:** consistent fonts, colors, and spacing across all pages signal professionalism and reduce the cognitive cost of navigating the report
- **Visual aids amplify structure:** labelled charts, annotated graphs, and clear section dividers reinforce the reading order without requiring explanatory text

## Why It Matters

A structurally sound report does not need a guided tour. Users can navigate it independently because the layout itself communicates the hierarchy of information. Structural integrity is a prerequisite for [[data-storytelling]] — you cannot tell a coherent story with a disorganised layout.
