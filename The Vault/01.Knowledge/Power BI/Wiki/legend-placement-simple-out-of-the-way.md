# Legend Placement — Simple and Out-of-the-Way

Legends must be minimal and positioned where they do not obstruct any visual data.

## Principle

A legend explains the visual encoding (color, shape, size) so users can read the chart. But the legend itself is not data — it is scaffolding. When a legend is large, cluttered, or positioned over the chart, it competes with the actual data and reduces the effective canvas. The best legend is the one the user never notices because placement, size, and content are all correct.

## Guidelines

- Keep legend text short — abbreviate if necessary
- Position legends outside the chart area (bottom or right), never on top of data
- Hide legends on single-series charts where the title already tells the full story
- Group multi-series legends into logical categories rather than listing every series individually
- Never use a legend where a direct label on the data point is more readable (e.g., bar charts with few categories)
