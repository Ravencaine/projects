# We Replaced 47 Excel Files With One Power BI Model

> **Type:** article
> **Author:** Gulab Chand Tejwani
> **Published:** 2025-10-20
> **URL:** https://medium.com/power-bi-made-easy/we-replaced-47-excel-files-with-one-power-bi-model-heres-what-actually-happened-9c0a6b8c0d7h
> **Routed to:** Power BI

## Summary

A real-world case study of migrating a 47-Excel-file reporting ecosystem into a single Power BI model. Covers what worked, what failed, the organizational challenges (change management, user adoption), and the measurable outcomes.

## Key Claims

- Biggest challenge was NOT technical — it was organizational: convincing analysts to stop maintaining their own Excel files
- Shadow IT problem: analysts had built their own macros and formulas in Excel; Power BI broke their workflows
- Change management took 3x longer than the technical migration
- Measurable outcome: 40% reduction in time-to-publish monthly reports
- Key lesson: pilot with a receptive team first, not the most resistant

## Notable Details

- The 47 Excel files had accumulated over 8 years with no governance
- Each Excel file had its own "master" data tab with formulas pointing to raw data tabs
- Power BI semantic model replaced the shared data layer; Excel files were repurposed as manual data entry for edge cases
- Training program: 2-hour workshop + 1-month support channel (Teams)
