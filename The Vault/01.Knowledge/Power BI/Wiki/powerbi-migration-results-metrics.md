# Power BI Migration Results — Measured Metrics

## Time Savings

**Weekly hours on manual updates (before → after):**

| Department | Before | After |
|------------|--------|-------|
| Finance | 18 hrs | 2 hrs |
| Sales | 12 hrs | 1 hr |
| Operations | 8 hrs | 0.5 hrs |
| HR | 3 hrs | 3 hrs (kept in Excel) |
| **Total** | **41 hrs** | **6.5 hrs** |

**Time saved: 34.5 hrs/week = 1,794 hrs/year**

At $65/hr: **$116,610 saved annually**

## Error Reduction

**Monthly errors reported (before → after):**

| Period | Errors |
|--------|--------|
| Pre-Power BI (avg) | 12.3/month |
| Month 1 post-cutover | 3 |
| Month 2 post-cutover | 1 |
| Month 3 post-cutover | 0 |

**89% reduction in reported errors.**
