# Know Your Audience Before Designing the Dashboard

Every design decision flows from who will read the dashboard. A dashboard for a CFO looks nothing like one for an operations manager, even when they use the same underlying data.

Before picking a single chart, answer:
- Who is the audience?
- What decision will this dashboard support?
- What do they already know vs what do they need to see?
- What is their risk tolerance?
- What time do they have to read it?

If the audience is mixed, design for the primary decision-maker first, then layer detail for secondary audiences.
