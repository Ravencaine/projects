Welcome! Today, we’re diving into the latest addition to Power BI—the new button slicers introduced in the November 2023 updates. This feature brings a fresh way to interact with your data visuals, making it easier and more intuitive to filter information. Let’s break down what this new slicer is all about, how to use it, and how it can enhance your Power BI experience.

## What is the Button Slicer?

The button slicer is a new feature that allows users to make selections in a visually appealing way. Unlike traditional slicers, this one lets you hover over buttons that highlight when selected, offering a more dynamic interaction. Additionally, you can incorporate images into your buttons, making your reports not only functional but also visually engaging.

![New button slicer feature in Power BI](https://databear.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/f09d944b-52d2-417d-9804-572248745aa9.png)

## Getting Started with the Button Slicer

To begin using the button slicer, you’ll first need to set it up in Power BI Desktop. Here’s a step-by-step guide on how to do that:

1. Open Power BI Desktop and navigate to the **Visualizations** pane.
2. Look for the new button slicer icon and click on it to add it to your report.
3. Once added, you can start customizing it by dragging fields from your data model into the slicer.

For our demo, we used a table containing coffee names and their corresponding image URLs. Make sure to set your image URL data category correctly as a web URL to display images properly.

![Setting up the button slicer with coffee names](https://databear.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/e986f84d-fd58-4cd2-a56a-2903f38b4c25.png)

## Customizing Your Button Slicer

After adding the button slicer, you can customize its appearance and functionality:

### Formatting Options

- **Shape Selection:** Choose between rectangle or rounded rectangle shapes for your buttons.
- **Layout:** Decide how many rows and columns your buttons will occupy. You can opt for a single column with multiple rows or adjust as needed.
- **Overflow Settings:** Choose between continuous scroll or pagination for navigating through options.

![Customizing the button slicer layout](99.System/Attachments/Customizing_the_button_slicer_layout.png)

### Image Integration

You can also add images to your button slicer. Simply select your image field and adjust the fit to ensure it displays correctly within the button. This feature allows for a more personalized and branded report experience.

## Advanced Features

The button slicer comes with advanced settings that enhance user interaction:

- **Single vs. Multi-Select:** You can configure whether users can select multiple options or just one at a time.
- **Callout Values:** Customize how buttons appear when selected, hovered over, or pressed.
- **Button Effects:** Add visual effects like shadows and glow to make your buttons stand out.
