# DAX Measures are Preferred Over Calculated Columns

<!-- Calculated columns are evaluated at model load time, stored in memory, and do not respond to slicers; DAX measures evaluated at query time are almost always the correct choice. -->

## Claim

Calculated columns are evaluated at model load time, stored in memory, and do not respond to slicers; DAX measures evaluated at query time are almost always the correct choice.
