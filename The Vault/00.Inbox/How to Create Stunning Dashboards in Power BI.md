## Ways to Create Stunning Dashboards

### 1\. Consider the audience and their purpose

The following situations should be taken into account when you improve the dashboard layout for your audience:

- How does your audience utilise the dashboard?
- Which crucial metrics aid in decision-making for your audience?
- What ingrained beliefs or cultural presumptions could influence design decisions?
- What information is required to help your audience succeed?

Remember that the dashboard is just a summary and a single location to check the status of the data at that moment. It relies on semantic models and underlying reports, both of which frequently have a wealth of information.

### 2\. Select the correct data source

Power BI supports numerous data sources, and the quality of the data has a major impact on the quality of your dashboard. Make sure your data is clean, correct, and current, whether you’re extracting it from databases, social media sites, cloud services like Azure, or Excel spreadsheets.

You can learn how to use Power Query to clean and convert your data as well as the different types of data connectors by enrolling in a Power BI training course.

This is a crucial step because low-quality data might lead to erroneous results even in visually appealing dashboards.

### 3\. Make sure to use effective data visualisation

Selecting the appropriate data visualisation is essential. Numerous visualisations are available in Power BI, like maps, bar charts, line graphs, scatter plots, pie charts, and more.

Every one of these visualisations has a distinct function, and choosing the appropriate one will improve your dashboard’s readability and efficiency.

**The best methods for selecting visualisations:**

- **Bar and Column Charts:** Perfect for evaluating changes over time or comparing categories.
- **Line charts:** Line charts are an effective tool for displaying trends over time, such as increases in website traffic or sales.
- **Pie charts:** When showing portions of a larger image, use them sparingly. Pie charts that have too many segments may be challenging to read.
- **Maps:** These are great for showing geographic data, such as sales by region or locations of clients.
- **KPIs:** Key performance indicators are a useful tool for assessing your progress toward particular company objectives.

### 4\. Uses filters and slicers

Power BI is an effective solution for exploratory data analysis since it enables real-time dashboard interaction. Users can delve down into particular data subsets using filters and slicers, which are crucial tools that keep users on the dashboard.

**For example,** users can filter data based on a period, product category, or area using a slicer on a sales dashboard. Filters and slicers allow users to explore the data that most directly pertains to them by increasing flexibility and improving dashboard interactivity.

![users can filter data based on a period, product category, or area using a slicer](99.System/Attachments/users_can_filter_data_based_on_a_period,_product_category,_or_area_using_a_slicer.jpg)

### 5\. Try to make it simple

Simplicity is essential when creating a dashboard design. It’s critical to keep in mind that a dashboard’s main function is to transmit facts understandably and efficiently.

Overloading the viewer with information on your dashboard might cause confusion and overload. Rather, concentrate on a small number of essential KPIs that most benefit your audience.
