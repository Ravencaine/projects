--------|-------------|
| Mean | Arithmetic average — sum divided by count |
| Median | Middle value — 50% above, 50% below |
| Mode | Most frequently occurring value |
| Variance | Spread — average of squared deviations from mean |
| Standard Deviation | Square root of variance — most common dispersion measure |

## Inferential Statistics

Draws conclusions about an entire **population** from a **sample**.

### Key Principle

> For a sample to be representative, members must be selected **randomly**: every population member has the same chance of inclusion.

### Sample Size and Margin of Error

```
Margin of Error = 1 / √(sample size)
```

As sample size increases, margin of error decreases (but at a diminishing rate — halving the error requires 4× the sample size).

### The Big Data Promise

Traditional: sample because surveying the entire population is economically infeasible.

Big data promise: process the entire population directly — no sampling, no margin of error.

## Normal Distribution (Bell Curve)

In a normal distribution:
- **Mean = Median = Mode** (all at the center)
- 68.27% of values within ±1 standard deviation
- 95.45% within ±2 standard deviations
- 99.73% within ±3 standard deviations (Three Sigma Rule)
