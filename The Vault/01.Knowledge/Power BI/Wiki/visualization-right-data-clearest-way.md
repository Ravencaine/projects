# Visualization: Right Data in the Clearest Way

The foundational principle of effective data visualisation in Power BI — and the most frequently violated by beginners.

## The Core Rule

> **Great visualisations are not about showing ALL your data. They're about showing the RIGHT data in the CLEAREST possible way.**

Most first Power BI reports fail because they show everything and communicate nothing. Every visual should answer a question, not display a dataset.

## Three Tests for Every Visual

Before keeping any chart or visual, ask:

1. **What specific question does this answer?**: If you can't name it in one sentence, the visual is unfocused.
2. **Can it be understood in 5 seconds or less?**: A senior executive should grasp the insight without reading a single label. If it takes longer, the visual is too complex for its purpose.
3. **Does it lead to a clear insight or action?**: The visual should end with "so we should…" or "which means…" — not just "and here's some data."

## The Beginner Mistake

You spend hours cleaning your data. You drag every field onto the canvas. The result: a confusing rainbow of bars, lines, and colours that tells the audience nothing they couldn't find by opening the spreadsheet.

> **Clean data is only half the battle.** The other half is knowing how to transform that data into visuals that communicate insights, not just data.

## Practical Application

For every visual you create:
- Start with the business question, not the data
- Select only the fields that directly answer that question
- Remove every element that doesn't serve the answer (extra legends, gridlines, axis labels for decorative series)
- Ask a colleague: "What do you see?" — the answer tells you whether the visual is clear
