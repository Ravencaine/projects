# Mary-Jo Diepeveen

AI expert at Microsoft focused on educational content around data and AI.

> **Type:** book
> **Sources:** 1 book in the vault

## Bio

Mary-Jo Diepeveen is an AI expert at Microsoft who focuses on creating educational content around data and AI. Her passion for AI stems from her experience in neuroscience, where collecting and analysing brain data to understand unconscious behavioural patterns led to an interest in machine learning and AI. This interest has continued to grow during her years at Microsoft, and she is frequently invited to speak and inspire others to get involved in tech.

## Known For

- Explaining AI/ML concepts to citizen data scientists and Power BI practitioners
- Making Azure Cognitive Services and Azure ML accessible without requiring data science expertise
- Connecting neuroscience-inspired thinking to practical machine learning applications
- Author of *Artificial Intelligence with Power BI* (Packt Publishing, 2022)
