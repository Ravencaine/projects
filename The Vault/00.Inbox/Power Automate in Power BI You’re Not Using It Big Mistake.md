If you’re using **Power BI** but haven’t tapped into **Power Automate**, you’re missing out on one of the biggest productivity boosts in data analytics.

Automation isn’t just a luxury — it’s a necessity in today’s fast-moving business world. **Power Automate + Power BI = fewer manual tasks, faster insights, and seamless workflows.**

Still not convinced? Here’s what you’re losing by not using them together.

## 1\. What Happens When You Combine Power BI + Power Automate?

Power BI gives you insights. **Power Automate makes those insights actionable.**

Imagine:  
✅ **Reports that refresh and email themselves**  
✅ **Alerts when your data hits critical thresholds**  
✅ **Auto-updated dashboards without lifting a finger**

This isn’t futuristic — it’s **available right now**, and you’re probably still doing it manually.

## 2\. 5 Power Automate Use Cases You Should Be Using

## 1️⃣ Auto-Refresh & Share Reports

**Problem:** Manually exporting and emailing reports every Monday.  
**Solution:** Power Automate triggers a **data refresh + PDF export + email delivery** on schedule.

## 2️⃣ Real-Time Alerts for Anomalies

**Problem:** Missing sudden sales drops or inventory shortages.  
**Solution:** Set up **automated email/SMS alerts** when KPIs go out of range.

## 3️⃣ Sync Data Across Apps

**Problem:** Updating Excel → SharePoint → Power BI separately.  
**Solution:** Automatically push **Power BI data to Excel, Teams, or CRM systems**.

## 4️⃣ Approval Workflows

**Problem:** Chasing managers to sign off on reports.  
**Solution:** Automate **approval requests via Teams/Outlook** with reminders.

## 5️⃣ Auto-Backup Your Datasets

**Problem:** Losing hours of work due to crashes.  
**Solution:** Schedule **daily backups to OneDrive/SharePoint**.
