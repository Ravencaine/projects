# Data Storytelling — Why Humans Need Narrative

Humans are wired for stories, not statistics. Effective data communication requires narrative context alongside raw numbers.

## Definition

Data storytelling is the practice of framing data insights within a narrative: who is the subject, what happened, why it matters, and what to do about it. Even with accurate models and clear visualisations, a story makes the insight actionable.

## Key Points

- A 70% chance of rain is abstract; "take an umbrella today" is actionable
- Reports with narrative explanations are more likely to drive decisions than charts alone
- Power BI tooltips, tooltips pages, and narrative insights are all storytelling mechanisms
- The analyst's role is to translate data into a story stakeholders can act on

## Storytelling as a Dashboard Design Practice

Janvi Gupta's dashboard design checklist treats storytelling as a "bonus tip" outside the main checklist — but it is arguably the highest-order design principle. Every visual design choice (color, layout, information flow, titles, tooltips) either serves or undermines the story the data is trying to tell.
