# Crafting Data Narratives: The Art of Power BI Reporting

## Source

- **Article:** "Crafting Data Narratives: The Art of Power BI Reporting"
- **Author:** [[Author-Isabelle-Bittar|Isabelle Bittar]]
- **Published:** 2024 (date not confirmed in source)
- **Source URL:** Medium / Microsoft Power BI publication
- **PBIX:** Available for download from article

## Summary

The article presents a 7-step process for designing Power BI reports that communicate clearly and support decision-making. The process draws from UX design, data journalism, and the Double Diamond design framework. Key themes include:

- Designing for the 3-30-300-second user journey (see [[The-3-30-300-Rule]]).
- Applying [[Visual-Design-Principles]] and [[Gestalt-Principles]] to dashboard layout.
- Using [[Visual-Information-Seeking-Mantira]] to sequence information at different detail levels.
- Applying [[Report-Stewardship]] practices post-publication.

## Key Extracts

### The 7 Steps
1. Identify the perspective (who is the audience?)
2. Define the question hierarchy (3/30/300 seconds)
3. Build a lo-fi wireframe
4. Identify data requirements
5. Develop the high-fidelity prototype
6. Test with real users
7. Iterate post-publication

### Design Philosophy
> "Power BI is not just a tool for displaying data — it is a medium for telling stories that drive decisions."

### Case Study
The article includes a full case study of an HR recruitment dashboard, showing how the 7 steps translate into a concrete report design.
