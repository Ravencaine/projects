Everyone wants to become a **Power BI Developer**.

They learn DAX.  
They build dashboards.  
They watch tutorials.

But after a few weeks… most people stop.

Why?

Because they miss the most important skill:

👉 **Consistency**

## What Consistency Means for a Power BI Developer

Consistency is not about building one perfect dashboard.

It’s about:

- Opening Power BI **every day**
- Practicing DAX regularly
- Improving one small concept at a time

Even when you don’t feel like it.

## 💡 The Reality Most Beginners Ignore

Many beginners think:

👉 “I’ll learn Power BI in 10 days”

But the truth is:

- DAX takes time
- Data modeling takes practice
- Real-world dashboards require experience

And the only way to build that?

👉 **Consistent effort over time**

## 📊 A Simple Comparison

Imagine two learners:

**Person A**

- Watches tutorials for 5 hours in one day
- Doesn’t practice for a week

**Person B**

- Practices 1 hour daily
- Builds small dashboards regularly

After one month:

👉 Person B becomes job-ready faster

## ⚙️ Where Consistency Shows Results

In Power BI, consistency compounds in multiple areas:

## 🔹 DAX Skills

Writing measures daily improves logic and speed

## 🔹 Data Modeling

Understanding relationships becomes natural over time

## 🔹 Visualization

You start designing cleaner, more professional dashboards

## 🔹 Problem-Solving

You begin to think like a real analyst

## 🚧 Why Most People Fail

Let’s be honest.

Consistency is hard because:

- You don’t see results immediately
- Errors in DAX can be frustrating
- Learning feels slow in the beginning

So people quit.

## 🔄 The Power of Small Daily Wins

Instead of aiming big, focus small:

- Learn **1 DAX function per day**
- Build **1 small visual daily**
- Solve **1 real-world problem regularly**

👉 These small wins turn into **big skills**

## 🎯 Practical Routine (Example)

If you’re serious about becoming a Power BI Developer:

- 20 mins → Learn a concept
- 20 mins → Practice (DAX / modeling)
- 20 mins → Build something

👉 Just **1 hour daily = massive growth**

## ⚡ Rule to Follow

👉 **Never skip two days in a row**

Missing one day is okay.  
Breaking the habit is not.

But becoming good at it requires repetition.

The developers who succeed are not the smartest.
