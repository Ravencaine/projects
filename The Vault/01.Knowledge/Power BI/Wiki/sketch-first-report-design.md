# Sketch-First Report Design

Low-fidelity paper sketches → high-fidelity Figma drafts before touching Power BI. Focus on story frames and insights, not on displaying all requested KPIs.

## Phases

1. **Low-fidelity sketch** — paper or Figma, rough layout, emphasis on narrative flow
2. **Quick data glance** — Power BI, basic connections, drag-and-drop charts; no data modeling investment
3. **Inspiration collection** — Dribbble, Behance, Figma Community, Envato
4. **Higher-fidelity sketch** — Figma, refining shapes, text, images
5. **Color + typography** — apply brand palette and fonts
6. **Socialize WIP** — gather feedback from end-users, not just BI devs

## Key Principle

Concentrate on creating frames that tell a story and offer insights rather than showcasing all requested KPIs. Sketch as if preparing to present — design for the narrative.

## Don't

Jump straight into Power BI and let data trends drive the design — this leads to a data dump, not a report.
