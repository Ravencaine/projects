# Multi-Row Card as KPI Summary

Display key performance indicators (KPIs) in a single, scannable view — Total Sales, Profit, Customer Count, Average Order Value.

## Purpose

Multi-row cards condense multiple KPIs into one visual without requiring a chart. Ideal for executive dashboards and overview pages where stakeholders need a snapshot of business health at a glance.

## When to Use

- Executive summary pages
- Landing/dashboard home pages
- Context panels beside drill-through pages
- Any page needing a high-level status at a glance

## Design Notes

- Use data labels and consistent number formatting
- Limit to 4–6 metrics per card to maintain readability
- Pair with conditional formatting (color-coded variance, trend arrows) for faster signal extraction
