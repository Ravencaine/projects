When building dashboards in Power BI, it can be challenging to finalize a design that meets stakeholder expectations before development. This is where **power bi wireframing** can help. Using a design tool like Figma, you can create a clear wireframe that maps out the layout and look of your dashboard before implementing it in Power BI.

In this article, Sekou Tyler from Lovelytics explains how to wireframe Power BI dashboards with Figma, and why it makes your development process more efficient.

##### What is Wireframing in Power BI

Wireframing is a design step where you plan the structure, layout, and content of a dashboard in a mockup tool before building it. Instead of starting directly in Power BI, you design a static draft that shows where visuals, text, filters, and other elements will appear.

Figma makes this process easier because it offers pixel-precise canvases, reusable color palettes, easy grouping, and fast editing. It allows you to test and refine designs quickly with your team and stakeholders.

##### Why Use Figma for Power BI Wireframing

Here are key reasons to use Figma for wireframing Power BI dashboards:

- Match your canvas size in Figma to your Power BI report dimensions, such as 1280 by 720 pixels for a 16:9 layout.
- Group elements logically so you can adjust and duplicate them easily.
- Define and apply your corporate brand colors within the design.
- Share drafts with stakeholders for quick feedback and approval.
- Export approved wireframes and use them as a guide or background in Power BI Desktop.

##### Steps to Wireframe a Power BI Dashboard in Figma

##### Set up the canvas

Start by creating a frame with dimensions that match your Power BI canvas. For a standard 16:9 report, use 1280 by 720 pixels.![Set up the canvas Figma for Power BI Wireframing ](99.System/Attachments/Set_up_the_canvas_Figma_for_Power_BI_Wireframing_.png)

##### Build the layout

Place rectangles, text boxes, and shapes to represent charts, slicers, KPIs, and other visuals. Include logos, headers, and any other brand elements.![Build the layout](99.System/Attachments/Build_the_layout.png)

##### Add color and style

Apply your organization’s color scheme to the elements. Figma allows you to define and reuse colors consistently.

##### Iterate and refine

Share the wireframe with stakeholders. Adjust the design based on their feedback. Making changes at this stage is much faster than editing a finished Power BI report.

##### Export and implement

Export the approved wireframe as an image or SVG. You can use it as a guide or as a canvas background when building the report in Power BI Desktop.![Export and implement](https://databear.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Screenshot-2025-07-06-120709.png)

##### Best Practices and Tips

Avoid embedding critical text in the background image so that it remains accessible and editable in Power BI. Use Figma’s grouping and duplication tools to speed up layout creation. If your team already uses PowerPoint for mockups, note that Figma is more powerful and flexible for this purpose.

Figma has a free plan for individuals and paid plans for teams. Evaluate which plan works best for your project.
