## Covariance

Measures how much **two random variables change together**. Positive covariance = they tend to move in the same direction. More sensitive to scale than correlation.

## Exponential Smoothing

Like a **moving average** used to forecast the next value in a time series. Assigns exponentially decreasing weights to older observations.

## Descriptive Statistics (Full Output)

The Descriptive Statistics tool outputs: Mean, Standard Error, Median, Mode, Standard Deviation, Sample Variance, Kurtosis, Skewness, Range, Minimum, Maximum, Sum, Count.
