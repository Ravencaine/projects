# White Space — Strategic Separation

Use white space deliberately to separate elements and improve readability; avoid overcrowding the dashboard.

## Principle

White space (the empty area between elements) is not wasted space — it is a design element. Gutters, padding, and gaps between visuals give the eye natural stopping points. Overcrowded dashboards force the eye to work harder to isolate individual elements, increasing cognitive load. This is a direct application of [[Gestalt-Principles]]: proximity groups related elements; white space signals where groups begin and end.

## Guidelines

- Add consistent gutters (minimum 8–16px) between all visuals
- Never place charts edge-to-edge — the lack of breathing room signals confusion
- White space is most valuable between conceptual groups, not between every individual element
- A crowded dashboard is a signal to split across multiple pages, not to compress
