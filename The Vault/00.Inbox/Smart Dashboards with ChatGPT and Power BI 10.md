## Chapter 6 Learning Module — Predictive Analytics and Sentiment Analysis in Power BI

Welcome to the future of dashboarding.

In this Chapter 6 Learning Module, we go beyond visualizing what’s already happened — we show you how to **forecast what’s next**. Using **predictive analytics**, **real-time sentiment analysis**, and **ChatGPT-enhanced modeling**, this module will teach you to build dashboards that think, plan, and respond dynamically.

Whether you work in **manufacturing**, **agriculture**, or **investment analytics**, the skills you’ll build here will elevate your dashboard from descriptive to predictive.

## Why Predictive Dashboards Matter

Data is only valuable if it tells you what to do next.

By combining **Power BI** with **ChatGPT’s natural language capabilities**, you can:

- Build custom forecasting models in seconds.
- Extract sentiment from text and translate it into strategy.
- Predict future outcomes using real-time signals — not just past reports.

## What You’ll Master in Chapter 6:

## 1\. Manufacturing Cost Forecasting Dashboard

**Use Case:** Chip manufacturing companies managing volatile raw material prices.

- Predict future material costs using OpenAI-powered modeling.
- Build DAX measures to calculate supplier risk and purchase timing.
- Automate Python-based API calls to ChatGPT to simulate future pricing.
- Visualize trends using side-by-side actual vs. predicted charts.

*Result:* Smarter procurement, cost avoidance, and data-backed timing strategies.

## 2\. Agricultural Demand Forecasting Dashboard

**Use Case:** Farmers and ag-tech teams planning planting and harvest schedules.

- Integrate weather, crop, and market sentiment data into Power BI.
- Use ChatGPT to predict crop demand based on rainfall, temperature, and buyer sentiment.
- Visualize correlations between weather patterns and yields with heatmaps and scatter plots.
- Design dashboards that provide alerts for optimal planting and harvesting windows.

*Result:* Reduced waste, improved yield timing, and future-ready decisions.

## 3\. M&A Deal Success + Stock Price Forecasting Dashboard

**Use Case:** Investors and analysts tracking major technology mergers.

- Predict M&A deal success probabilities using ChatGPT insights.
- Forecast stock price changes based on regulatory impact and sentiment.
- Use real-world case data (e.g., Intel acquiring Qualcomm) for realistic simulation.
- Visualize sentiment trends over time and highlight decision points using custom visuals.

*Result:* Deeper market analysis, faster trading decisions, and competitive edge.
