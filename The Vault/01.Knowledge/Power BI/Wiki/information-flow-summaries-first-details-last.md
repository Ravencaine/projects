# Information Flow — Summaries First, Details Last

Arrange visuals in a logical sequence that guides users from high-level summaries at the top to granular details at the bottom.

## Principle

Cognitive flow matters: users who land on a dashboard need orientation before detail. Placing the most important KPI cards and summary charts at the top — and drilling into detail progressively as the eye moves down — mirrors how people read a document. This principle is embedded in the [[Visual-Information-Seeking-Mantira]]: overview first, zoom and filter, then details on demand.

## Reading Order Guidelines

1. **Top-left to bottom-right**: the natural reading path — place the most important visuals near the top-left
2. **Summary before detail**: trend charts and KPI cards at top; supporting tables and raw data at bottom
3. **Grouped by decision**: metrics that answer one question belong together; do not scatter them across the page
4. **Mobile consideration**: test the vertical reading order on mobile — the top-to-bottom flow is the only reading order on small screens
