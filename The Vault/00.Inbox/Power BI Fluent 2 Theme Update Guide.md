The **Power BI Fluent 2** theme introduces a major upgrade to how reports look and feel in Power BI. Instead of the outdated default styling, this new update brings modern visual defaults and cleaner design principles that make dashboards more professional with minimal effort. In this guide, you’ll learn how to enable the Fluent 2 theme, what changes it brings, and how it improves your Power BI report design.

##### Enabling the New Power BI Preview Features

To access the new Fluent 2 theme and modern visual improvements, you need to enable a preview feature in Power BI Desktop.

##### Steps to Enable Fluent 2 Theme

1. Open **Power BI Desktop**
2. Go to:
	- **File → Options and Settings → Options**
3. Navigate to:
	- **Preview Features**
4. Scroll until you find:
	- **Modern visual defaults and customizing theme improvements**
5. Tick the checkbox
6. Click **OK**
7. Restart Power BI Desktop

After restarting, the new theme options become available.

##### Exploring the Fluent 2 Theme in Power BI

Once Power BI restarts, you can access the new theme system:

- Go to the **View** tab
- Open **Theme Setup**
- Click **Customize Current Theme**

You will now notice a new option:

##### Fluent 2 Theme

This is part of Microsoft’s broader effort to modernize the Power BI interface and bring consistency across Microsoft products.

When applied, the Fluent 2 theme immediately transforms report visuals into a more polished and modern design.

##### Why Fluent 2 Makes Such a Big Difference

The improvement is not subtle it’s visual and structural.

##### Key enhancements include:

- Cleaner default spacing
- More modern typography
- Improved color harmonization
- Better visual hierarchy
- Reduced “out-of-the-box clutter”

Instead of reports feeling like legacy dashboards, they now resemble modern SaaS analytics platforms.

This is especially important for:

- Business dashboards
- Executive reporting
- Client-facing analytics

##### Testing the Theme Across Different Reports

One of the most interesting findings is how the Fluent 2 theme behaves across different report types.

##### 1\. Existing Custom-Themed Reports

When applied on top of a custom theme (e.g., community or “Guy in a Cube” style themes), the result remains visually strong. The Fluent 2 layer enhances consistency without breaking design structure.

##### 2\. Clean Analytical Reports (Example: Oslo City Bike Analysis)

In analytical dashboards, the Fluent 2 theme immediately improves readability.

Benefits include:

- Better chart clarity
- Improved whitespace usage
- More professional visual alignment

Even simple datasets look significantly more refined without manual formatting.

##### 3\. Complex Business Models (Example: AdventureWorks)

For enterprise-level models like AdventureWorks:

- The theme automatically updates multiple visuals
- Some pre-existing design decisions may be overridden
- Layout consistency improves significantly

However, there is a trade-off:

> If you’ve heavily customized your report, switching themes may override intentional styling choices.

##### Should You Switch to Fluent 2 Theme?

This is an important consideration.

##### When it makes sense:

- New reports
- Early-stage dashboards
- Standard reporting templates
- Quick analytics projects

##### When you should be cautious:

- Highly customized enterprise reports
- Brand-specific dashboards
- Reports with strict design guidelines

In those cases, you may prefer staying with a classic theme or selectively applying Fluent 2 elements.

##### Reverting Back to Classic Theme

If you don’t like the new look, Power BI allows you to revert easily:

- Open **Theme Setup**
- Select:
	- **Classic 2026 setup**
- Click **Apply**

This instantly restores the previous visual system.

##### New Bonus Feature: Camera Settings in Power BI

Alongside the Fluent 2 theme, another useful enhancement is introduced in the **Format tab → Camera settings**.

##### What’s new?

You can now control report display size options, including:

- HD
- Full HD
- 4K resolution support

##### Why this matters:

- Better presentation quality for stakeholders
- Improved large-screen dashboard clarity
- More flexibility for report sharing and embedding

This is especially useful for executive dashboards displayed on large monitors or presentations.

##### Final Thoughts: A Big Step Forward for Power BI Design

The Fluent 2 theme update is more than a cosmetic change it represents a shift toward:

- Modern UI consistency
- Faster report design
- Reduced need for manual styling
- Better default user experience

For many users, this will significantly reduce the time spent making reports “look good” and increase time spent on **actual data insights**.
