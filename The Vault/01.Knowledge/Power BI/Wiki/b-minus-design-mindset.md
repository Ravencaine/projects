# B-Minus Design Mindset

If your sketch at this stage is a B- deliverable, you are doing great — don't chase perfection in early design phases.

## The Trap

Spending excessive time on the last 20% of a sketch, only to find the requirements change once Power BI development begins. "The last 20% can easily take 90% of the time/resources."

## The Practice

- Spread the design process over multiple days
- Take breaks between sessions — fresh eyes catch more than polished effort
- Set a quality bar at "good enough to communicate the concept" rather than "ready to ship"
- Iterate: low-fidelity → medium-fidelity → high-fidelity, not one-shot to final

## Framing

"B- changes the world." — progress over perfection in the sketch phase.
