## Summary

Five general Power BI mistakes targeting beginners: calculated column overuse, poor star schema, too many visuals per page, ignoring performance optimisation, and improper slicer/filter use. The article frames Power BI development as experience design — building for decision-making, not information density.

## Key Claims

1. Calculated columns bloat the model — measures are dynamic and preferred
2. Star schema is the correct modeling pattern — messy models cause cascading problems
3. Visual density hurts comprehension and performance — 5–7 focused visuals per page
4. Performance optimisation must happen early — unused columns, complex DAX, Import vs DirectQuery
5. Slicers and filters make reports interactive — without them reports are read-only dashboards
