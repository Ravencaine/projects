## 1\. Create Visuals and Reports with Natural Language

You can now describe what you want, and Copilot will generate charts, visuals, and even full report pages.

Example: “Show me a bar chart of monthly sales by region in 2024” Copilot will analyze the dataset, select appropriate fields, and generate the visual for you.

## 2\. Generate DAX Measures and Calculations Automatically

Writing DAX can be complex. With Copilot, you can describe the logic, and it will create the correct DAX formula.

Example: *“Create a measure for year-over-year revenue growth”* Copilot outputs a ready-to-use DAX measure with proper formatting and logic.

## 3\. Summarize and Explain Data Insights

Copilot can generate textual summaries, bullet points, or narratives explaining trends, anomalies, or key drivers.

Example: *“Summarize sales performance by product category”* Useful for dashboards, stakeholder updates, or automated business narratives.

## 4\. Ask Questions in Natural Language (Q&A Enhancement)

You can interact with your data just like chatting — Copilot improves the Q&A experience by offering more accurate, context-aware responses, even guiding you to refine your questions.

Example: *“What was the highest selling product last quarter?”*

## 5\. Auto-Generate Report Descriptions and Documentation

Copilot helps write page summaries, titles, and report documentation, saving time when publishing or sharing reports with others.

Example: *“Write a summary of this report for executives”*
