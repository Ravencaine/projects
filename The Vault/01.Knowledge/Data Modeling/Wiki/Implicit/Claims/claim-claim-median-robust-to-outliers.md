# Median is Robust to Outliers

<!-- When outliers are genuine (not data entry errors), the median should be used for central tendency instead of the mean, since the median is not distorted by extreme values. -->

## Claim

When outliers are genuine (not data entry errors), the median should be used for central tendency instead of the mean, since the median is not distorted by extreme values.
