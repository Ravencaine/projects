# Retail Sales KPI Framework

A structured set of key performance indicators that give executives an at-a-glance view of retail business health across financial, operational, and customer dimensions.

## Definition

The framework groups KPIs into categories: financial performance (Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin), operational activity (Total Orders, Average Order Value), and customer/geographic performance (top customers, regional breakdown, category sales).

## Key Points

- **Financial KPIs** answer "are we making money?" — Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Discount Rate.
- **Operational KPIs** answer "are we active?" — Total Orders, Average Order Value, Sales per Region.
- **Product KPIs** answer "what is selling?" — Top Products, Category Sales, Category Profit, Sales by Sub-Category.
- **Customer KPIs** answer "who is buying?" — Customer Segment breakdown, Top Customers by Revenue.
- **Temporal KPIs** answer "are we growing?" — Monthly/Quarterly/Yearly Sales Trend, Year-over-Year growth.
- A well-designed executive dashboard presents KPIs in order of business priority, not chart type.
