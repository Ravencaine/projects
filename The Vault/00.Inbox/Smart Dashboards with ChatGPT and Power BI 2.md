## Chapter 5 — Building Smart Dashboards: Power BI and ChatGPT in action

**This chapter covers:**

· **Building and customizing Power BI dashboards with dynamic data**

· **Utilizing ChatGPT to streamline DAX guidance and automation**

· **Creating interactive dashboards for sports analytics and other domains**

· **Enhancing dashboard performance and responsiveness with AI assistance**

In Chapter 4, we explored the art of dashboard design, focusing on how ChatGPT can elevate the process through creative visualization, storytelling, and efficient data handling. We explored key design principles — such as visual hierarchy, clarity, and simplicity — transforming raw data into actionable insights. ChatGPT’s role was pivotal in generating innovative ideas for layout design, optimizing color schemes, and customizing dashboards for specific audiences. Additionally, we demonstrated how ChatGPT can streamline the ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) process, ensuring data is clean, accurate, and ready for analysis. Through engaging case studies — including real estate management, customer satisfaction tracking, and a food truck business — we showcased how the integration of ChatGPT with Power BI enhances creativity, improves efficiency, and supports data preparation, resulting in impactful, data-driven dashboards that guide informed decision-making.

In this chapter, you’ll dive deep into the technical world of Power BI, learning how to integrate dynamic data sources, create custom visualizations, and use DAX for advanced analytics — all with ChatGPT simplifying even the most complex tasks. Whether you’re tracking athlete performance in sports analytics, monitoring financial trends, or visualizing church / non-profit data, you’ll discover how AI can supercharge your workflow, allowing you to focus on delivering powerful insights with confidence.
