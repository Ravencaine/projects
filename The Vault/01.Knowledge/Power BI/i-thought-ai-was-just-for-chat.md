Sign in to your XDA account I used to treat AI like a glorified search engine — something to help me draft a quick email or summarize an article. That changed when I started integrating AI directly into my Office suite. It all started when I hit a Tuesday afternoon deadline with 40 rows of messy data in Excel and a 12-slide PowerPoint deck that needed to be finished by EOD. Instead of going through manual formatting and VLOOKUPs, I let Copilot take charge. I ended up with a workflow that did the job in 20 minutes instead of the usual three to four hours. Unlock AI in all Office apps Requires a relevant Microsoft 365 plan For a long time, if you wanted the pro version of AI inside your spreadsheets and slides, you had to pay an extra $20 every month for a separate Copilot Pro subscription. It felt like a luxury add-on. But Microsoft recently flipped the script by bundling Copilot directly into the standard Microsoft 365 plans. Now, I don’t have to manage a separate AI subscription anymore. It’s now just a native part of the MS365 suite. If you are part of the Personal plan or an admin of the Family plan, look for the Copilot icon appearing in the top right corner of Word, PowerPoint, OneNote, and Excel. There was a slight price hike to the base plan to accommodate the rollout. Comparing a marginal price bump to the old $20 standalone fee feels like a massive win for anyone who was already curious about AI. If you are on a Family plan, the AI features are tied to the primary subscription owner. My family members still get the standard apps and storage, but the AI brain stays with the admin account. Using Copilot in Microsoft PowerPoint From pixel pushing to visual storytelling Close Here is how I have shifted my entire PowerPoint workflow from manual design to AI orchestration. I don’t start by picking a font or dragging text boxes anymore. I have found that the more specific I am with the prompt, the better the output. Instead of just saying ‘make a deck about retirement,’ I will use a targeted prompt like below. Create a presentation about financial planning to retire before 50. Keep the deck to around 10 slides and focus on aggressive saving rates and diversified portfolios. Within seconds, Copilot outlines the narrative, creates the titles, and gives a basic overview of the upcoming presentation. I can glance over it to make sure the logic flows. If I need a specific vibe, I will tell Copilot to use a professional design template. I can even ask the AI to generate unique images or pull high-res assets directly from the Microsoft 365 library. After the AI generates the deck, I don’t just leave it as-is. I use the Design Ideas tool to flip through different layouts for each slide. If a slide feels too text-heavy, one click on a Designer suggestion transforms it into a sleek timeline or a high-impact visual. Now, I can go in to make small tweaks like adjusting the tone of a specific bullet point, swapping one chart for another, or adding a personal touch. The difference is that I’m spending 90% of my time on the message and only 10% on the formatting. Exploring Copilot in Excel Instant pattern recognition When I first saw the Copilot icon in my Excel ribbon, I was skeptical. After all, I have spent years mastering VLOOKUPs and Pivot Tables — could an AI really handle my data better than the cloud? The answer is yes. For example, instead of trying to remember the exact syntax for a nested IF or an XLOOKUP, I just describe what I want in plain English: Calculate a 15% bonus for every sales rep who exceeded their target in Column D, but only if they are in the Enterprise region. Copilot generates the formula, explains exactly how it works, and then asks if I want to insert a column. If I’m looking at a spreadsheet with thousands of rows, I don’t start by building a Pivot Table. I start by asking questions like these. What were the top three drivers of growth this quarter compared to last year? Give me the list of top-performing salespersons from the North region. The possibilities are endless here. I highly recommend exploring Copilot in Excel to speed up your workflow. Automate the Office suite like a pro You don’t need to be a prompt engineer or a data scientist to make this work. You just need to stop asking AI to write and start asking it to do. I recommend taking that one recurring Excel report or that slide deck you dread building and let Copilot take the first pass.
