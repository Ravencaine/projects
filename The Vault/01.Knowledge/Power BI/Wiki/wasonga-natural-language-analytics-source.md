## Summary

NLA lets users ask data questions in plain English and receive answers as charts, KPIs, tables, or AI-generated explanations. Key distinction: NLQ (Natural Language Query) retrieves data; NLA (Natural Language Analytics) analyses it and adds context. Covers the technology stack (NLP, query translation, automated viz selection, NLG), key platform capabilities, per-user value, and Bold BI's three AI features (AI Agent, AI Copilot, Smart Narrations). BARC data: only ~25% of employees actively use BI tools despite wide investment — NLA closes that gap.
