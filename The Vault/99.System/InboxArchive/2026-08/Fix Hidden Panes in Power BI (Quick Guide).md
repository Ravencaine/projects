If you need to **fix hidden panes** in Power BI or Power Query, you’re not alone. Missing items like the Formula Bar, Applied Steps pane, or Filters panel can slow down your workflow. The good news? These features aren’t deleted they’re simply hidden, and you can restore them in just a few clicks.

Maybe your **Formula Bar disappeared**.  
Maybe the **Applied Steps pane is gone**.  
Or perhaps your **Filters, Data, or Visualization panes** vanished in Power BI Desktop.

The good news? You didn’t delete them they’re just hidden.

In this guide, you’ll learn exactly how to restore hidden panes in Power Query and Power BI Desktop using simple, built-in options.

##### Why Do Panes Go Missing in Power BI?

Most missing panes happen because:

- They were accidentally turned off in the **View tab**
- The layout was modified
- The modern pane switcher was customized
- A chevron (expand/collapse arrow) was clicked

Fortunately, everything can be restored in seconds.

##### Fixing Hidden Panes in Power Query Editor

Let’s start inside **Power Query Editor**.

##### 1\. Restore the Queries Pane (Using the Chevron)

If your **Queries pane** is missing:

- Look for the small **chevron (arrow)** on the left side
- Click it to expand the pane
- Your list of queries will instantly reappear

This is the quickest fix and often the most overlooked.

##### 2\. Bring Back the Applied Steps Pane

Missing your **Applied Steps**?

Here’s how to restore it:

1. Go to the **View** tab
2. Under the **Layout** section
3. Click **Query Settings**

When you select **Query Settings**, the **Applied Steps pane** reappears on the right side.

Many users accidentally close it and assume it’s deleted but it’s simply hidden.

##### 3\. Turn the Formula Bar Back On

The **Formula Bar** displays your **M code**, which is essential when working with transformations.

If it’s missing:

1. Go to the **View** tab
2. Check the **Formula Bar** option

Once enabled, your M expressions will appear again.

This is especially important for advanced Power Query users who write or modify M code manually.

##### 4\. Missing Parameter Options? Enable Parameters

If you’re creating a **Conditional Column** and don’t see the option to use parameters:

1. Go to the **View** tab
2. Enable **Parameters**

Once allowed, your parameters will be available when building conditional logic or dynamic transformations.

##### Fixing Hidden Panes in Power BI Desktop

Now let’s switch to the **Power BI Desktop** experience.

Power BI uses a modern pane switcher that allows you to customize what appears on the right-hand side.

##### Understanding the Modern Pane Switcher

You may also need to fix hidden panes in Power BI Desktop’s modern interface.

Power BI uses a customizable pane system that includes:

- Filters
- Data
- Build (Visualizations)
- Format
- Bookmarks
- Selection
- Performance Analyzer
- Sync Slicers

When any of these disappear, you can fix hidden panes using the steps below.

##### 1\. Use the Pane Manager to Restore Hidden Panes

The Pane Manager is your main control center.

To fix hidden panes:

1. Go to the **View** tab
2. Select **Pane Manager**
3. Toggle the panes you want visible

You can turn everything off and re-enable only what you need.

##### 2\. Too Many Panes? Resize or Minimize Them

Sometimes you don’t need to fix hidden panes you need to manage too many open ones.

You can:

- Use the chevron to minimize
- Use the ellipses (…) to close
- Resize panels manually

This keeps your report canvas clean and organized.

##### Pro Tip: The View Tab Is Your Best Friend

Whenever something is missing in:

- Power Query Editor
- Power BI Desktop
- Microsoft Fabric

Always check the **View tab first**.

Nearly every hidden pane issue can be fixed there.

##### Common Hidden Pane Fix Checklist

If something is missing, check:

- ✅ View → Query Settings (Applied Steps)
- ✅ View → Formula Bar
- ✅ View → Parameters
- ✅ Chevron expanders
- ✅ View → Pane Manager (Power BI Desktop)

This quick checklist will solve 95% of layout issues.
