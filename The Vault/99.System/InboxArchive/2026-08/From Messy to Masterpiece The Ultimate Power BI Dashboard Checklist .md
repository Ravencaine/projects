**Why Does Dashboard Design Matter?**

Dashboard design is crucial because it directly impacts how well viewers understand the data. A poorly designed dashboard, cluttered with information and lacking visual hierarchy, can leave users confused and overwhelmed.

Conversely, a well-designed dashboard with clear visuals, consistent formatting, and a logical layout allows users to quickly grasp key insights, identify trends, and make informed decisions based on the data presented. It’s the difference between deciphering a cryptic message and reading a well-organized report.

**“ This checklist empowers you to create dashboards that are not only informative but also visually appealing and user-friendly. ”**

## Visual Design: A Feast for the Eyes

![](99.System/Attachments/1!bAosCAF2Su11gTqErSa1rw.png.webp)

**⒈ Color Palette:**

- **Limited & Focused:** Stick to 3–5 colors for a clean look.
- **High Contrast:** Ensure colors are easily distinguishable, especially for accessibility. Consider using a color contrast checker.
- **Consistent Representation:** Apply colors consistently across visuals to represent similar data points.

**⒉** **Fonts: Professional & Readable**

- **Professional Choice:** Select a professional font family that aligns with your brand identity.
- **Hierarchy & Readability:** Consider using separate fonts for headings and body text to improve hierarchy and readability.
- **Emphasis Consistency:** Maintain consistent boldness or italics for emphasis throughout the dashboard.

**⒊ Background Matters: Subtle & Supportive**

- **Non-Competing Colors:** Use a subtle background color that doesn’t compete with the data visualizations.
- **Brand Alignment (Optional):** Consider a simple background based on your brand for visual interest, but ensure it doesn’t overpower the data.

## Visual Formatting: Making Sense of the Data

![](99.System/Attachments/1!JsqiuF87_5VVscrwoh12jQ.png.webp)

**⒈ Visual Size: Highlight Key Metrics**

- **Focus & Clarity:** Utilize larger visuals for key metrics or focus areas.
- **Consistent Comparison:** Maintain consistent sizing for visuals of the same type to facilitate comparison.

**⒉ Title (Header): Clear & Concise**

- **Informative Titles:** Craft concise and informative titles that accurately reflect the data presented.
- **Consistent Case:** Use title case or sentence case for consistency.

**⒊ Labels: Units & Readability**

- **Meaningful Units:** Format data labels with appropriate units (e.g., percentages, currency).
- **Truncation & Tooltips:** Truncate long labels with ellipses (…) and provide full details on hover.
- **Emphasis Consistency:** Maintain consistent boldness or italics for emphasis throughout the dashboard.

**⒋** **Rounding Numbers: Striking a Balance**

- Apply consistent rounding rules throughout the dashboard.
- Consider the level of detail needed and avoid excessive decimal places.

## Dashboard Layout: Organization is Key

![](99.System/Attachments/0!GoZcQHPMZrCdgzDQ.jpg.webp)

**⒈ White Space: Breathing Room for Insights**

- **Strategic Separation:** Utilize white space strategically to separate elements and improve readability.
- **Avoiding Clutter:** Avoid overcrowding the dashboard.

**⒉ Alignment & Grid System: A Structured Foundation**

- **Structured Layout:** Align elements horizontally, vertically, or both for a structured layout.
- **Consistent Balance:** Consider using a grid system to ensure consistent spacing and balance.

**⒊ Grouping: Categorize for Clarity**

- **Related Visuals:** Group related visuals or sections based on topic or data type.
- **Visual Dividers:** Use headers or dividers to separate these groups visually.

**⒋ Ordering: A Clear Information Flow**

- **Logical Sequence:** Arrange visuals and sections in a logical order that guides users through the information flow.
- **Prioritize Summaries:** Prioritize high-level summaries at the top and progress to details.

## Additional Considerations: The Finishing Touches

- **Tooltips:** Provide concise and relevant information in tooltips (data points, percentages, etc.).
- **Legends (if needed):** Keep legends simple and strategically placed to avoid obstructing visuals.
- **Interactivity:** Consider enabling slicers, filters, or drill-down capabilities for user interaction and deeper analysis.
- **Export Options:** Ensure the dashboard can be easily exported to different formats (PDF, image).
- **Abbreviation Explanations:** Explain any abbreviations used (e.g., ETA = Estimated Time of Arrival).
- **Focus on Key Headers:** Only display essential visual headers.
