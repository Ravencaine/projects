# Power BI Subscriptions for Hidden Pages

> **Type:** article
> **Author:** Gulab Chand Tejwani
> **Published:** 2025-10-03
> **URL:** https://medium.com/power-bi-made-easy/how-to-set-up-power-bi-subscriptions-for-hidden-pages-9e8e6b8c0d3f
> **Routed to:** Power BI

## Summary

Power BI subscriptions can deliver reports from hidden pages — enabling automated distribution of detail views, supporting data exports, or executive summary-only reports without exposing the full report to all viewers.

## Key Claims

- Hidden pages in Power BI are not visible to report consumers but can still be subscribed to
- Useful for: raw data exports (hidden data tables), executive-only views, behind-the-scenes detail
- Subscription recipients do not need access to the full report — they only receive the subscribed page
- Attachments (PDF, PPTX) include only the subscribed page, not the full report
- Schedule frequency: daily, weekly, or on a specific day/time
