# Turn Chart Title Into an Insight

<!-- use the title to communicate the key takeaway, not just the subject — people scan visuals -->

## Definition

Replace generic chart titles (e.g., "Turnover Rate by Department") with insight-bearing titles that communicate the key takeaway at a glance, including current values and period-over-period change.

## Key Points

- People do not read charts — they scan them; the title is the first thing they see
- Dynamic titles (built with DAX) can show the current KPI value and how it has changed since the prior period
- A color cue in the title (e.g., red for increase, green for decrease) reinforces the direction visually
- The title can be built in SVG and rendered via the Image visual for full styling control

## Examples

Static → Insight-bearing:

- Before: "Turnover Rate by Department"
- After: "Turnover increased by 2.3% to 14.7% since last month"
