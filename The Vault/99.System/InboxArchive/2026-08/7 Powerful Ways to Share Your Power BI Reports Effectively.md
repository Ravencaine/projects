## ✅ 1. Share via Power BI Service (Workspace & App)

- Best for: Internal users within your organization.
- How: Publish the report to a workspace. Share via a Power BI App or directly share the report link.
- License needed: Power BI Pro (for both sender and viewer), or Premium capacity (for larger orgs).

## ✅ 2. Share Report Link (with Access Permissions)

- Best for: Sharing with colleagues.
- How: Click Share > Enter email addresses of users > Send.
- Control access with options like “Allow reshare”, “Build permission”, etc.
- License: Power BI Pro or Premium.

## ✅ 3. Publish to Web (Public)

- Best for: Blogs, public dashboards, or non-sensitive info.
- How: In Power BI Service, choose File > Publish to web. Generate an iframe/embed code.
- ⚠️ Not secure — anyone with the link can view.
- License: Free or Pro.

## ✅ 4. Embed in a Website or Application

- Best for: Portals, apps, or secure internal dashboards.
- How: Use Power BI Embedded (requires Azure and developer setup). Or embed in SharePoint Online with Power BI web part.
- License: Power BI Pro, Premium, or Power BI Embedded (via Azure).

## ✅ 5. Export as PDF or PowerPoint

- Best for: Offline sharing or presentations.
- How: Click File > Export > PDF or PowerPoint (PPTX).
- Limitations: Not interactive; static content.
- License: Free or Pro.

## ✅ 6. Schedule Email Subscriptions

- Best for: Regular updates to users.
- How: Go to the report > Subscribe > Set frequency. Sends report snapshots via email.
- License: Pro or Premium.

## ✅ 7. Share with External Users (B2B)

- Best for: Clients, vendors, or partners outside your org.
- How: Share with external emails (they need to log in with Microsoft account). Set proper guest user permissions via Azure AD B2B.
- License: Power BI Pro for sharer, and Pro or Premium capacity for viewer.
