## Claim

When aggregating scores from multiple reviewers, geometric mean naturally reduces the disproportionate influence of extreme outlier scores because it works on multiplicative relationships. Example: scores of 20 and 80 produce a geometric mean of 40 vs. arithmetic mean of 50.
