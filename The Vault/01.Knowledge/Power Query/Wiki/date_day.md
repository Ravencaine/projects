# Date.Day

Returns the day component of a date, datetime, or datetimezone value. dateTime: A date, datetime, or datetimezone value from which the day component is extracted. Example Get the day component of a date, datetime, or datetimezone value representing the date and time of 5/14/2011 05:00:00 PM.
