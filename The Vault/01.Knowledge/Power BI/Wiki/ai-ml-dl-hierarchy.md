# AI ⊇ ML ⊇ DL Hierarchy

The three terms form a nested containment relationship — DL is a specialised form of ML, which is a specialised form of AI.

## Definition

```
AI  ⊇  ML  ⊇  DL

AI  = Everything that makes machines act intelligently
ML  = Training models from data instead of explicit rules
DL  = Deep neural networks with many layers for complex, unstructured data
```

## Key Points

- AI is the **umbrella term**: it includes approaches that don't use ML at all (rule-based systems, expert systems)
- ML sits inside AI: it trains models from data rather than being explicitly programmed
- DL sits inside ML: it uses deep neural networks for tasks where feature engineering is impractical
- The hierarchy is important for scoping: not every AI problem needs deep learning

## Examples

- AI (rule-based): a chatbot with hard-coded if/then response rules
- ML (classical): logistic regression on tabular sales data to predict churn
- DL: convolutional neural network identifying objects in product photos
