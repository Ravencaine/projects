**This chapter covers:**

1. Defining Data Visualization: Discover how to transform raw data into dashboards.

In this chapter, we uncover the transformative power of dashboards and generative AI, revealing how these cutting-edge tools can revolutionize data analysis and decision-making. This chapter aims to inspire and equip you with the knowledge to harness the full potential of Power BI and generative AI, transforming raw data into actionable insights and driving innovation in your field.
