Meta-level: stop writing "10 tips" DAX posts because patterns without context are dangerous. ALLSELECTED cardinality trap (47s → 1.2s), SUM vs SUMX misconception, 95% of Power BI Medium content is pattern-matching without context. Shifts to context-first writing: cardinality-aware, failure stories, debugging methodology.
