# Pie and donut charts should have at most 4-5 slices

<!-- Beyond 4-5 slices, pie and donut chart labels become unreadable and the eye cannot accurately compare slice sizes. If there are more categories, switch to a horizontal bar chart sorted by value. -->

## Claim

Beyond 4-5 slices, pie and donut chart labels become unreadable and the eye cannot accurately compare slice sizes. If there are more categories, switch to a horizontal bar chart sorted by value.
