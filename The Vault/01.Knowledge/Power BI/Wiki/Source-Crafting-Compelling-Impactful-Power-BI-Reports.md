## Summary

Six practical principles for elevating Power BI reports beyond technical correctness: structural clarity, data-driven narratives, interactive features, data accuracy, tooltip design, and user empowerment. The article frames report creation as data storytelling, drawing a parallel to the analyst's role in translating numbers into actionable insight.

## Key Claims

1. **Structural Integrity:** coherent report structure (intro → body → formatting consistency) is the foundation for clarity
2. **Data-Driven Narratives:** use titles, captions, and annotations to guide viewers through the data story
3. **Interactive Features:** drill-down, filters, and slicers empower users to explore tailored insights
4. **Data Accuracy and Reliability:** validating sources and regular data cleansing are prerequisites for trust
5. **Optimise Tooltips:** tooltips present supplementary detail without cluttering visuals
6. **Empower Users Through Interactivity:** self-service exploration increases report engagement and utility
