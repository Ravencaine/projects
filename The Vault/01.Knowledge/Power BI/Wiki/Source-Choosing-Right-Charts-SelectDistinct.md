## Summary

Beginner guide to matching chart type to analytical goal. Covers: line charts (trends over time), bar/column charts (comparing groups), pie/donut charts (parts of a whole), scatter plots (relationships between two numeric variables). Secondary topics: using slicers and tooltips to reduce clutter, avoiding 3D effects, limiting pie slices to 4–5 maximum.

## Key Principles

- Pick the chart that fits your **goal**, not the most colourful option
- Reduce clutter: remove heavy gridlines, avoid shadows and decorations
- Use colour purposefully — consistent colours signal important changes
- Slicers > multiple charts for filtering by category
- Tooltips keep main visual clean while preserving detail on demand

## Chart Decision Summary

| Goal | Chart Type |
|------|-----------|
| Show change over time | Line chart |
| Compare groups | Bar / column chart |
| Show parts of a whole | Pie / donut chart (≤4–5 slices) |
| Find relationships between two numeric variables | Scatter plot |
