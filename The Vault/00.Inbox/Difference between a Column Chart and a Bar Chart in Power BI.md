## 📊 1. Column Chart

- Orientation: Vertical
- X-Axis: Categories (e.g., Product Names, Departments)
- Y-Axis: Values (e.g., Sales, Profit)
- Best For:

🟩 *Example*: Displaying monthly sales across a year.

## 📉 2. Bar Chart

- Orientation: Horizontal
- X-Axis: Values
- Y-Axis: Categories
- Best For:

🟦 *Example*: Comparing revenue across 15 different countries.
