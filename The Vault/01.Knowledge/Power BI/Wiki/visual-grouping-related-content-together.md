# Visual Grouping — Related Content Together

Group related visuals by topic or data type and use visual separators (headers, dividers) to reinforce the groupings.

## Principle

The eye naturally groups elements that are physically close together or share visual features (Gestalt proximity and similarity). Leveraging this by placing related charts and KPIs in distinct visual zones reduces the time it takes for users to orient themselves. Each group should tell a coherent part of the story — revenue together, operational metrics together, workforce data together.

## Guidelines

- Name each group with a section header or divider
- Leave a clear gap between groups to reinforce separation
- Do not mix unrelated data types within a group — resist the temptation to fill every available space
- Use background containers (Power BI shapes) to visually box groups on a single page
