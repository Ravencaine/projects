## Summary

Color in dashboards is a communication tool, not decoration. Intentional color use guides attention; accidental color use misleads. Five principles for effective color use: be selective, maintain consistency, think inclusivity, consider the mood, brand wisely. The 3-30-300 rule defines dashboard reading hierarchy: 3 seconds (overview), 30 seconds (insights), 300 seconds (details).
