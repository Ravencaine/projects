# AI Democratisation in Power BI

Power BI brings AI to non-data-scientists through pretrained models, built-in visuals, and Azure Cognitive Services — no data science degree required.

## Definition

AI democratisation means making AI accessible to anyone who wants to use it, regardless of technical background. Power BI achieves this by embedding AI capabilities directly into the report-building workflow: built-in visuals that apply ML automatically, one-click AI Insights that call Azure Cognitive Services, and Azure ML integration without writing code.

## Key Points

- **Lack of data science skills** is the #1 barrier to AI adoption
- Power BI's out-of-the-box features require no code, no model training, no ML expertise
- Azure Cognitive Services are pretrained models — accessible via a subscription key and endpoint
- The barrier to AI has shifted from "can we build it?" to "do people know it exists?"
- Power BI democratises AI at three levels: built-in visuals → Cognitive Services → custom ML

## Why It Matters

Without democratisation, only data scientists can use AI. With it, the analyst building the sales dashboard can also add forecasting, anomaly alerts, and text analytics — without waiting for an ML team.
