# Greg Deckler

Greg Deckler is a Microsoft MVP and one of the most influential voices in the Power BI and DAX community.

> **Type:** book
> **Sources:** 1 book in the vault

## Bio

Greg Deckler is a prolific author, blogger, and community contributor focused on Power BI, DAX, and business intelligence. He is known for challenging conventional DAX wisdom through his "CALCUHATE" philosophy — the argument that CALCULATE is inessential to DAX and that a No CALCULATE approach is more learnable and maintainable.

## Known For

- **CALCUHATE movement:** Challenging the DAX community's reliance on CALCULATE
- **DAX For Humans:** A ground-up DAX curriculum that teaches fundamentals without starting with CALCULATE
- **Microsoft MVP:** Recognized Microsoft expert in Power BI
- **Community contributions:** Active forum contributor, conference speaker, GitHub author
