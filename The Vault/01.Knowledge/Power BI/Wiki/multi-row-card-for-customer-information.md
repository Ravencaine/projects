# Multi-Row Card for Customer Information

Show complete customer context — Name, Email, Phone Number, Loyalty Tier, Lifetime Value — when a user selects a customer in a report.

## Purpose

Enables CRM-style dashboards within Power BI. Gives sales and account teams a full customer snapshot without leaving the report or navigating to an external CRM.

## When to Use

- Customer analytics dashboards
- Sales performance reports
- Account management or customer success reports

## Design Notes

- Link to customer selection via drill-through or selection sync
- Format Lifetime Value with currency and two decimal places
- Loyalty Tier benefits from a categorical color scale or badge formatting
