## 1\. Analyzing Relationships Between Two Variables

- Use Case: See how one metric impacts another.
- Example: Analyze the relationship between advertising spend and sales revenue.

📊 Scatter charts help you answer:

> *“As advertising increases, does revenue also increase?”*

## 2\. Trend and Correlation Analysis

- Use Case: Identify positive, negative, or no correlation between metrics.
- Example: Compare employee experience (years) with performance scores.

> Visualize clusters or lines to assess correlation direction.

## 3\. Identifying Outliers

- Use Case: Spot data points that don’t fit the usual pattern.
- Example: In sales data, quickly find regions with high expenses but low sales.

> Useful for risk assessment and anomaly detection.

## 4\. Multi-Dimensional Analysis (Using Bubble Size & Color)

- Use Case: Add more insights with size and color for a 4D view.
- Example: X-axis: Profit Y-axis: Revenue Bubble Size: Customer Count Color: Region

> This makes it perfect for executive dashboards.

## 5\. Time-Based Movement (Play Axis Feature)

- Use Case: Track how data points evolve over time.
- Example: Monitor product performance over several quarters.

> The “Play Axis” animates movement across time — great for storytelling.

> *When to Use Scatter Charts in Power BI*

> You want to compare two numeric variables
> 
> You need to spot clusters or patterns
> 
> You’re doing regression, correlation, or anomaly analysis
