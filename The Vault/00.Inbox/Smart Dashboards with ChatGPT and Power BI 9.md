**Chapter 5 Learning Module —Building Smart Dashboards: Power BI and ChatGPT in Action**

Welcome to the most advanced hands-on module in your Smart Dashboards journey. In this lesson, we focus on building **real-time, high-performance dashboards** powered by the secret sauce behind Power BI: **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions).**

With ChatGPT by your side, you’ll learn how to generate, customize, and optimize DAX formulas that transform your dashboards from static visuals into intelligent, responsive tools.

**Why DAX Matters — and How ChatGPT Makes It Easier**

Power BI is only as powerful as the DAX formulas behind it. DAX controls how data is calculated, filtered, and displayed — but writing complex formulas can be time-consuming and frustrating.

This module shows you how to:

1). Use **ChatGPT to generate optimized DAX** for time series analysis, performance tracking, and KPI calculations.  
2). Transform business questions into **automated logic** — without needing to memorize every DAX function.  
3). Build dashboards that **respond in real time** and deliver insights with precision.

**What You’ll Master in the Chapter 5 Learning Module:**

**1\. Real-Time Stock Portfolio Dashboard**

- Connect to **live stock APIs** and use DAX to calculate metrics like **volatility**, **moving averages**, and **total returns**.
- Let ChatGPT write and debug your formulas, saving hours.
- Build dashboards that update automatically and track performance over time.

**2\. Sports Marketing Dashboard for Stadiums**

- Analyze how **promotions impact attendance and in-game revenue** using DAX-powered calculations.
- Create measures like **avg. spend per attendee**, **promo lift %,** and more.
- Use ChatGPT to enhance dashboard layout and logic for fan engagement strategies.

**3\. Church / Nonprofit Analytics Dashboard**

- Use DAX to track **donation trends**, **campaign effectiveness**, and **engagement over time**.
- Create custom KPIs (e.g., **donation per member**, **event ROI**) that help leaders make better decisions.
- Let ChatGPT optimize your dashboard flow for storytelling and clarity.

**4\. Football Analytics Dashboard**

- Track **yards per play**, **success rate on 3rd downs**, **play calling efficiency**, and other key metrics — using DAX measures you’ll learn to write and refine.
- Use ChatGPT to fine-tune visuals and ensure your data loads fast, even with complex filters.
