### 📊 What Are Calculation Groups in Power BI?

**Calculation Groups** in Power BI are a way to simplify and organize your measures. Instead of creating many similar measures like “Total Sales YTD”, “Total Sales QTD”, and “Total Sales MTD”, you can create just one measure and use a calculation group to switch between different time calculations.

Think of calculation groups as a smart menu that helps Power BI know *how* to calculate a value, without creating many separate formulas.

### 💡 Why Use Calculation Groups?

- ✅ **Less Repetition**: No need to create lots of similar measures.
- ✅ **Better Organization**: Keep your model clean and easier to manage.
- ✅ **More Flexibility**: Quickly switch between different calculations like Year-To-Date (YTD), Quarter-To-Date (QTD), and Month-To-Date (MTD).

### 📁 Where Are They Created?

In Power BI Desktop when you have a local model open, navigate to Model view and select the Calculation group button in the ribbon. If you’re not already in Model explorer, the Data pane opens to the Model view.
