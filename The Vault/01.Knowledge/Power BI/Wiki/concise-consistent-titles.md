# Concise, Consistent Titles

Dashboard titles must be informative and short, using a consistent capitalization scheme across the entire report.

## Principle

A title is the first context a user gets before interacting with a visual. It must communicate what the visual shows without requiring the user to interpret or infer. Inconsistently cased titles (Sentence case in one chart, Title Case in another) signal sloppy craftsmanship and reduce trust.

## Guidelines

- Keep titles to one line where possible
- Use sentence case or title case — but apply it uniformly across all visuals
- Avoid vague titles like "Chart 1" or "Sales" — name the specific measure and dimension
- Good: "Total Revenue by Product Category" — immediately tells the reader what they will see
