## Claim

Dimension groups in D365 F&O (e.g., Donated=Colour+Style+Size, DonatedNS=Colour+Style, DonatedS=Style, Retail Kit=Config) determine which attribute columns must match for pricing resolution, requiring conditional CASE/CONCAT joins.
