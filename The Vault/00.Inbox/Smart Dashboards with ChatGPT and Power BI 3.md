## Chapter 6 — Enhancing Dashboards with ChatGPT: Predictive Analytics and Sentiment Analysis in Power BI

**This chapter covers:**

· **Building and customizing Power BI dashboards** with **predictive analytics** and **real-time data**

· **Using ChatGPT** to assist with **predictive models** and **sentiment analysis** across **M&A, manufacturing**, and **agriculture**

· **Creating dashboards** for **forecasting stock movements**, **optimizing manufacturing costs**, and **predicting crop demand**

· **Improving decision-making** with **AI-driven insights** for more accurate forecasts and responsiveness

In Chapter 5, we explored the technical process of building smarter, data-driven dashboards using Power BI, with ChatGPT as a powerful assistant to streamline the workflow. We learned how to create dynamic, real-time dashboards across domains like sports, finance, and non-profits, allowing users to gain actionable insights by connecting to live data sources. ChatGPT was central in guiding users through DAX functions, automating complex calculations, and providing suggestions for optimal visual layouts, making the dashboard creation process more efficient and insightful. The chapter covered both foundational and advanced DAX functions such as **SUM**, **AVERAGE**, **CALCULATE**, and **FILTER**, which allow users to perform detailed data analysis and uncover deeper insights. ChatGPT demonstrated its ability to assist users at all levels, from beginners learning the basics of DAX to advanced users optimizing complex queries for better performance. ChatGPT’s role extended beyond automation, helping identify new metrics to track, improving dashboard performance, and refining visual clarity for better decision-making.

In this chapter, you’ll explore how AI transforms Power BI dashboards through predictive analytics and sentiment analysis. You’ll learn to build dynamic dashboards that forecast trends, optimize decision-making, and track real-time data. Whether you’re evaluating stock movements in technology M&A, managing raw material costs in chip manufacturing, or forecasting agricultural demand, ChatGPT will streamline predictive modeling and insights, enabling you to deliver more accurate and impactful dashboards.
