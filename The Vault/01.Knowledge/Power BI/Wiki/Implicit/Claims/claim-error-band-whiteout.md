# Error bands can be repurposed as white-out masks to hide chart regions and enable background images

<!-- Setting an error band's fill to solid white (0% transparency) and binding its upper/lower bounds to inflated values that extend beyond the data range paints over chart regions. This lets decorative background images show through, enabling oblique, letterboxed, or shaped chart looks. -->

## Claim

Setting an error band's fill to solid white (0% transparency) and binding its upper/lower bounds to inflated values that extend beyond the data range paints over chart regions. This lets decorative background images show through, enabling oblique, letterboxed, or shaped chart looks.
