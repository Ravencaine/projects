# From Messy to Masterpiece: The Ultimate Power BI Dashboard Checklist

> **Type:** article
> **Author:** Janvi Gupta
> **Published:** 2024-03-20
> **URL:** https://medium.com/@janvisgupta/from-messy-to-masterpiece-the-ultimate-power-bi-dashboard-checklist-1b42e57dc939
> **Routed to:** Power BI

## Summary

A structured 14-item dashboard design checklist covering three categories: Visual Design (color, typography, background), Visual Formatting (sizing, titles, labels, rounding), and Dashboard Layout (white space, alignment, grouping, information flow). Ends with finishing touches covering tooltips, legends, interactivity, and export.

## Key Claims

- Color palette: 3–5 colors, high contrast for accessibility, consistent representation
- Typography: professional font family, separate fonts for headings/body, consistent emphasis
- Background: subtle, non-competing with data; optional brand alignment
- Visual sizing: larger for key metrics, consistent sizing for same-type visuals
- Titles: concise and informative, consistent case
- Labels: meaningful units (%, currency), truncate long labels with ellipses, full details in tooltips
- Rounding: consistent rules across dashboard, avoid excessive decimal places
- White space: strategic separation of elements, avoid overcrowding
- Alignment: align horizontally/vertically, use a grid system for consistent spacing
- Grouping: group related visuals by topic, use headers/dividers for visual separation
- Information flow: logical sequence, high-level summaries at top, details at bottom
- Tooltips: concise and relevant information
- Legends: simple, strategically placed to avoid obstructing visuals
- Interactivity: slicers, filters, drill-down for deeper analysis
- Export: PDF, image formats
- Abbreviations: explain all abbreviations used
