## Summary

A 30-day practitioner account of integrating all of Power BI's native AI features (Copilot, Q&A, Key Influencers, Anomaly Detection, Smart Narratives, Python ML) into a real retail client dashboard. Covers what worked, what failed, the licensing realities, and the career implications for analysts.

## Key Claims

- AI in Power BI saves 12–17 analyst hours/week on Q&A and reporting tasks
- Copilot excels at root-cause analysis when the data model is clean
- Key Influencers reveals hidden correlations that aren't visible in standard reports
- Python integration with scikit-learn enables mid-80% accuracy forecasting
- Most AI features require Premium or PPU licensing — a real barrier for small clients
- AI shows correlations, not causations — analyst judgment remains essential
- Entry-level analyst tasks are being automated; senior analyst skills increase in value

## Notable Details

- The 60/40 split rule: spend 60% of time on data modeling, 40% on AI features
- Python forecasting used `LinearRegression` with features: Month_Num, Week_of_Year, Promotion_Active
- Anomaly detection sensitivity of 70–80% was the recommended starting point
- Q&A visual improves via the "Teach Q&A" synonym feature
- Employee X / Store 12 example: AI found a staffing correlation, not a causal employee factor
