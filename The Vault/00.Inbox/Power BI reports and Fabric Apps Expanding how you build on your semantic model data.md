Power BI is business intelligence, taking your data from stored numbers to measures and visualizations telling you what happened and helping you decide your next step. In the age of AI, we’re using these new capabilities to help you do even more with your data.  
  
Power BI semantic models remain essential for bringing together data from hundreds of sources and defining the logic that connects it, measures performance, and supports business decisions. From this semantic layer foundation, you can build reporting solutions for a wide range of scenarios.

Power BI reports is a reporting experience dedicated to your Power BI sematnic models. We handle the complexity of query generation so you can focus on creating visualizations and sharing them with others with options for integration with Office, the web, or your own portal. We’ll keep improving Power BI reports and visuals based on your ideas.

With AI, we’re also making it easier to create reports and models through modeling MCP and agentic skills report authoring, using our continued investments in PBIP and PBIR formats and the Desktop bridge to show these edits as you prompt. The new Power BI Desktop bridge lets secure local external edits appear directly in Desktop, so you can review and co-create with clicks. And our custom visuals in Power BI reports are now easier than ever to build with AI.  
  
Power BI reports aren’t the only way to work with semantic model data. You can also connect through Excel, Fabric notebooks, or other reporting tools to run DAX queries and create reports however you prefer. It’s your data, and our role is to give you secure, governed tools to combine it and surface it effectively. That includes custom web apps as well. With Rayfin, you now have a new AI-first way to build fully functional managed web apps on top of your semantic model data in just minutes.

The idea of building apps on top of your data is not new, but AI now makes it far more accessible and Fabric becomes your go-to hosting option. Rayfin is a secure way to leverage these powerful resources with AI. Your developer’s Fabric Apps can sit alongside your analyst’s Power BI reports, data scientist's notebooks, and many other reporting experiences to support a truly data-driven organization.

These options give you the tools you need to get your job done, no matter how you want to do it. Drag and drop visuals and fields in a Power BI report to get standardized visualizations with consistent themes you can reuse. Use prompts to get that exact look and functionality you want with Fabric Apps, leveraging the full scope of what CSS and TypeScript can do.

You can do all this while leveraging the same semantic model you’ve built—reusing your business logic pulling in data from Fabric with Direct Lake or import combining data sources together in a meaningful way.  
  
We’re excited to offer a full range of options for bringing data together, turning it into meaningful signals, and making it available to the people who need it. The result is a secure, governed ecosystem where people are empowered at every step to be more productive with AI and Copilot.
