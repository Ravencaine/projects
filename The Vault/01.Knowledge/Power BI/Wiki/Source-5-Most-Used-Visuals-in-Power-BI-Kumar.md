## Summary

A high-level guide to the five most-used Power BI visuals — bar chart, line chart, pie chart, card visual, and table/matrix — with guidance on when to use each one. Intended as a beginner introduction to visual selection.

## Key Claims

1. Bar Chart — best for comparing values across categories (East vs West vs North vs South)
2. Line Chart — best for tracking trends over time (monthly revenue growth)
3. Pie Chart — best for showing % contribution to a whole (market share), but avoid with too many categories
4. Card Visual — best for displaying a single KPI (Total Sales = ₹10M)
5. Table/Matrix — best for detailed data where users need exact numbers and drill-down

## Core Principle

The article's main message: great dashboards use the *right* visual at the right place, not every visual available.
