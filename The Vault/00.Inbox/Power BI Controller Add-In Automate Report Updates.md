**Power BI Controller add-in** automation is a game-changer for anyone embedding live Power BI reports into PowerPoint presentations. If you’ve ever spent hours manually updating slides each time your report changes, Microsoft has just made your life a whole lot easier.

With the new **Power BI Controller** for PowerPoint, you can manage all your embedded report pages in one centralized pane enabling **bulk updates, reconnections, and global settings changes** in just a few clicks.

##### What Is the Power BI Controller Add-In?

The Power BI Controller is a **task pane add-in** for PowerPoint that:

- **Detects all embedded Power BI report pages** in your presentation
- Allows you to **refresh or reconnect all report pages at once**
- Applies configuration changes across multiple slides
- Eliminates the need to go slide-by-slide to manage your reports

This is especially useful for data-driven presentations where content updates frequently daily, weekly, or even hourly.

##### How to Enable the Power BI Add-in in Your Tenant

Before you begin, make sure your organization has enabled the add-in:

1. Go to the **Power BI Admin Portal**
2. Search for “PowerPoint” in the search bar
3. Under **Tenant Settings > Export and sharing settings**, enable **Power BI add-in for PowerPoint**

> If disabled, this feature won’t work. Contact your IT admin to request access.![How to Enable the Power BI Add-in in Your Tenant](99.System/Attachments/How_to_Enable_the_Power_BI_Add-in_in_Your_Tenant.png)

##### Embedding Power BI Report Pages into PowerPoint

You can embed a report page in two ways:

##### Option 1: From Power BI Service

1. Open your report in PowerBI.com
2. Go to **Export > PowerPoint**
3. Select **Embed live data** for interactivity
4. Click **Copy Link**

Paste this into your PowerPoint using the Power BI add-in.

##### Option 2: Directly in PowerPoint

1. Open **Add-ins** in PowerPoint
2. Search for **Power BI**
3. Insert the **standard Power BI add-in**
4. Choose your report and select the specific page

Tip: Choose **live data** for interactive dashboards, or embed as image for a static snapshot.![Embedding Power BI Report Pages into PowerPoint](99.System/Attachments/Embedding_Power_BI_Report_Pages_into_PowerPoint.png)

##### Managing Permissions: Who Can View Embedded Reports?

Remember that the embedded Power BI content reflects **your access level**. If you share your deck with someone who doesn’t have access to the report:

- They **won’t see the data**
- You can choose to **grant automatic access** when embedding

This ensures the right people see the right data.

##### Resize and Customize Your Embedded Reports

Once embedded, the report behaves like a live visual inside PowerPoint:

- Resize the frame to fill the slide or share space with other visuals
- Click through visuals filters, slicers, and drill-through actions work as expected
- Use multiple pages from multiple reports across your presentation

##### Introducing the Power BI Controller Add-In (The Real Game-Changer)

The **Power BI Controller** is the second add-in you need to activate. You’ll recognize it by the gear icon next to the Power BI logo in the Add-ins menu.![Introducing the Power BI Controller Add-In](99.System/Attachments/Introducing_the_Power_BI_Controller_Add-In.png)

##### What It Does:

- Scans the entire presentation to detect **all embedded Power BI visuals**
- Lists them in a central **task pane**
- Allows you to **refresh**, **reconnect**, or **reset** all embedded visuals
- Applies configuration changes across multiple slides at once

Perfect for when you have **many embedded pages** and don’t want to update each one manually.

##### Key Features of the Power BI Controller Pane

Once installed and activated:

- It auto-discovers all Power BI add-ins in your deck
- You can apply settings to **sync with Power BI view** or restore defaults
- Easily reconnect visuals that lost authentication
- See last refresh times and get alerts if the data is outdated

> Save time managing embedded data without editing each slide!

##### Limitations and Considerations

- The Power BI Controller is still in **preview** (as of October)
- Large decks with many report pages may slow performance
- Both add-ins (standard + controller) must be installed for full functionality

##### Final Thoughts: A Must-Have for Data-Driven PowerPoint Users

If you’re embedding Power BI report pages into PowerPoint decks especially for executive dashboards, board reviews, or recurring reports the **Power BI Controller add-in** is essential.

It centralizes your report maintenance, saves hours of manual updates, and improves consistency across your presentation.
