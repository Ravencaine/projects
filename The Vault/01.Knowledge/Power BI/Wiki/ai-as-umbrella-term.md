# AI as Umbrella Term

AI is the broadest concept: any technique that lets a machine execute an intelligent task the way a human would.

## Definition

Artificial Intelligence (AI) encompasses all efforts to make machines perform tasks that require human-like intelligence — reasoning, learning, planning, perception, and language understanding.

## Key Points

- AI is the **umbrella term**: machine learning and deep learning sit beneath it
- The goal of AI is to find the best-performing model to predict new data
- Equally important (but often overlooked): understanding *how* to apply those models responsibly
- AI ≠ magic; it requires good data, a well-defined use case, and awareness of limitations

## Examples

- Power BI's built-in forecasting, anomaly detection, and Q&A visual
- Azure Cognitive Services for text and image analysis
- Custom models trained via Azure ML AutoML or Designer
