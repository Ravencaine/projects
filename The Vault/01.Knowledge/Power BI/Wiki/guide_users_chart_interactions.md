# Guide Users on Chart Interactions

<!-- most users miss drill-down, drill-through, expand hierarchy, and other interactions — add visual cues -->

## Definition

The majority of Power BI users do not discover chart interactions (drill down, expand hierarchy, drill-through, expand charts, click-to-navigate) without guidance. Adding a visual cue — via a text box or integrated into the chart subtitle — dramatically improves report usability.

## Key Points

- Common missed interactions: drill down, expand hierarchy, drill-through, expand charts, click to navigate to another page
- A short subtitle or text box ("Click to drill down by region") is often all that is needed
- This is a form of progressive disclosure — don't explain everything, just enough to lower the activation energy
- Particularly important for executive or self-service audiences unfamiliar with Power BI
