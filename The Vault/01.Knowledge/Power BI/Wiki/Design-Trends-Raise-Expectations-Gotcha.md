# Modern Design Trends Raise User Expectations — Gotcha

> **Type:** gotcha
> **Routed to:** Power BI
> **Primary source:** Isabelle Bittar — 2024-03-02

## Problem

Web and mobile app design trends constantly evolve. Users bring their expectations from everyday apps to the dashboards they use at work. Reports that don't match modern design expectations feel outdated or hard to use — even if the data is excellent.

## Why It Happens

- Users compare dashboards to the best consumer apps they use daily
- Power BI reports that look like "BI reports from 2010" feel inferior
- A complex or confusing dashboard damages user confidence in the underlying data

## The Trap

Report authors optimize for what they know (Power BI features) rather than what users expect (modern app UX).

## Key Expectations Shaped by Design Trends

| Trend | User Expectation |
|-------|------------------|
| Responsive design | Works on mobile and large screens |
| Bold typography | Text is easy to read, hierarchy is clear |
| Micro-interactions | Reports feel responsive and alive |
| Modern navigation | Nav is like a web app, not a static menu |
| Clean color palettes | Professional, not garish or cluttered |

## Impact

- Users prefer poorly-designed simple dashboards over well-designed complex ones
- Reports that "feel" modern get used more; reports that "feel" old get ignored
- Even accurate, valuable data is dismissed if the report looks unprofessional

## Solution

Stay current with web/app design trends and apply them to Power BI reports — especially navigation, typography, color, and responsiveness.
