## Chapter 8 Learning Module — Beyond the Dashboard: Future Trends and Technologies in Power BI and ChatGPT

**Future-Ready Dashboards: Streaming, Sentiment & Strategy with ChatGPT + Power BI**

Welcome to the final — and most advanced — learning module in the *Smart Dashboards with ChatGPT and Power BI* series.

Dashboards should do more than report the past. This module shows you how to forecast M&A impacts, simulate regulatory outcomes, analyze market sentiment in real time, and even deploy AR-enabled dashboards powered by conversational AI.

## Why This Module Redefines Your Workflow

Before AI, complex dashboards like this would take days or weeks to build — and you’d still be stuck manually toggling data sources, running calculations, or interpreting sentiment. Not anymore.

In this module, you’ll combine **real-time data feeds, technical trading metrics, and ChatGPT-powered logic** — all in Power BI.

## What You’ll Master in the Chapter 8 Learning Module:

## Streaming M&A Dashboard for Traders

- Monitor top media companies (e.g., Netflix, Disney+, Amazon) in real time.
- Simulate mergers using What-If parameters (synergy savings, regulatory risk).
- Integrate social sentiment and market indicators with live stock data.  
	**Time-Saving Tip:** Let ChatGPT generate merger impact summaries directly in Power BI. No reports required.

## Trading Risk Strategies + Decision Framework

- Use DAX to build VaR (Value at Risk), Bollinger Bands, and Beta volatility metrics.
- Add ChatGPT-generated analysis for regulatory approval odds.
- Integrate a “Decision Assistant” panel to guide risk-informed trades.  
	**Workflow Boost:** ChatGPT helps surface red flags before execution.

## AgTech Sentiment & Patent Opportunity Dashboard

- Combine patent activity, funding rounds, and R&D metrics with LLM-powered signals.
- Use AR to walk around a virtual “trading desk” and see sentiment overlaid live.
- Ask: *“Which AgTech startup saw the highest sentiment boost after Q1 funding?”* and get an instant visual + narrative.
- **New Capability:** Adaptive chart recommendations + natural language Q&A make insights faster than ever.
