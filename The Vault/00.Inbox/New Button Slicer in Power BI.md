In November Power BI update new button slicer was released for public preview. This new slicer experience expands customization possibilities to the next level.

The button slicer allows for the division of your design into rows and columns and uses pixels to customize card spacing. You can customize the shape of your design by changing the corner radius. With the button slicer, you can also infuse buttons with images, creating endless possibilities with URL images.
