# Socialize Work in Progress (Report Design)

Getting feedback early and often — from the right people — before finalizing a Power BI report design.

## Who to Solicit Feedback From

| Audience | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Analytically savvy end-users | Confirms the report answers the right questions |
| Non-technical stakeholders | Tests whether the narrative is accessible to the "lowest denominator" |
| Power BI developers | ⚠️ Use with caution — they tend to focus on technical elements, not what matters to end-users |

## When to Solicit

Don't wait for a finalized version. Present work-in-progress informally to catch direction errors early — before the Power BI build is complete.

## Why It Matters

Reduces rework. BI devs and report authors often disagree on what makes a good report — catching this in a sketch costs minutes; catching it after a full build costs hours.
