# Neil Dunlop

> **Type:** book
> **Sources:** 1 book in the vault

## Bio

Neil Dunlop is the author of *Beginning Big Data with Power BI and Excel 2013* (Apress, 2015). The book targets professionals who want to apply self-service BI tools to business problems without requiring Hadoop-scale infrastructure or data engineering expertise.

## Known For

- Positioning Power BI and Excel as the pragmatic "middle path" between raw data and enterprise BI platforms
- Integrating cloud services (Azure HDInsight, Azure Marketplace) into the Excel workflow
- Covering the full modern BI stack: data modeling, DAX, Power Query, Power View, Power Map, and HDInsight
- Emphasizing real-world case studies and cost-reduction examples over tool-feature documentation
