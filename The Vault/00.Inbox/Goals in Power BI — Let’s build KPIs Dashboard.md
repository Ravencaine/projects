One of the most interesting functionality in Power BI announced in 2021 are *Goals*. *Goals* add new possibility to define and share target KPIs defined above existing Power BI reports in Power BI cloud. *Goals* can be grouped into *Scorecards* where progress can be easily tracked, reviewed and commented.

These are main benefits of using *Goals* in Power BI:

## All KPIs in one place

Using *Goals Scorecard* you can create one dashboard that will include all KPIs that are important for your company. No need anymore to check many reports one by one to identify on which KPIs management should focus.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*AR39NWbzpgiucKUN8uLDvQ.png)

## Goals hierarchy

*Goals* hierarchy can be defined within the *Scorecard.* This simplifies orientation and navigation through more KPIs levels.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1278/format:webp/1*SZZ8RmY-4MwZE_0eqLpvTw.png)

## Goals responsibility

Responsible person can be assigned to each *Goal.* This person then can see all *Goals* that were assigned to him and managers can quickly identify who is the person with whom they should follow up if KPI is not on a good track.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*Akx_hpd2MRLPMHf1DMbbmg.png)

## Goals status

Each *Goal* in *Scorecard* has its status that can be customized for company needs. From the first look mangers will see which *Goals* are fulfilled and which ones are behind the plan.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*F92lqG023SwGnnyoNVCEBw.png)

## Goals Check-ins

*Goals Check-ins* allow users to update target values of specific *Goal*, change status or post comment. Comments can explain reasons why *Goal* is not fulfilled, or better, define actions that responsible person will take to return *Goal* on the track.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1378/format:webp/1*6N7MlCmV45qVDgD60Xd4vA.png)

Now let’s see how all these functionalities can work in real life.

## Use case scenario: Top-down approach

Using *Goals Scorecard* hierarchy of KPIs can be created and analysis can start from the top aggregated level and then continue up to the point when the root case is identified.

For example, in the first step manager will see that target profitability is not fulfilled, drilling to next level he will identify that the reason is lower total sales and drilling down to next level he will realize that sales quantity reached its target level but lower selling price is the main problem.

As next step, manger will continue to Selling Price Analysis report which will give him comprehensive analysis and help him to understand what are exact reasons why target selling price was not reached. In this case, reason of lower average selling price was lower sales volume of premium products with high selling price; it was impact of product mix.
