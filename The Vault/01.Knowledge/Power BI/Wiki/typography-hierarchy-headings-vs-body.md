# Typography Hierarchy — Headings vs Body

Use distinct font families or styles to create a clear reading hierarchy on dashboards.

## Principle

Separate visual weight between headings and body text so readers can scan the page structure before reading content. This is a standard UX pattern also used in [[Visual-Design-Principles]].

## Application

- Headings: larger, bold, or a distinct font family aligned with brand identity
- Body text: smaller, regular weight, designed for legibility at typical screen reading distances
- Emphasis: bold or italics used consistently throughout — never mixed arbitrarily
