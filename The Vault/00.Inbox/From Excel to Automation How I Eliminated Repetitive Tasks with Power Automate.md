For years, I thought Excel was my ultimate productivity tool.

I used it to track everything — from sales reports to attendance sheets and task lists. But somewhere along the way, the very tool that helped me stay organized became the source of **repetition, fatigue, and wasted hours**.

That’s when I found **Power Automate**.  
And things changed — fast.

## ⏱️ The Repetition I Couldn’t Ignore

Every Monday morning looked the same:

- Download a CSV from email
- Open Excel, clean the data
- Copy-paste into a master file
- Send updates to my manager via Outlook
- Archive the old version in SharePoint

It took **over an hour** — and it wasn’t even strategic work. It was just *manual labor* in digital form.

I started wondering: *Isn’t there a better way?*

## ⚙️ Discovering Power Automate

I’d heard of Power Automate as part of Microsoft’s ecosystem but never gave it much attention. One day, while browsing through the Power BI blog, I stumbled across a use case: **automating Excel-based workflows**.

That was the “aha” moment.

I logged into Power Automate and found something magical:  
A **template** called “When a file is added to OneDrive, extract data and send an email.”

Wait, what?

That’s exactly what I needed — just in reverse.

## 🧪 My First Flow: A Small Win with Big Results

Here’s what I built in less than an hour:

1. **Trigger:** When a new email arrives with an attachment
2. **Condition:** If it’s a `.csv` file
3. **Action 1:** Save it to OneDrive
4. **Action 2:** Use Excel Online to clean and format it
5. **Action 3:** Email a formatted summary to my manager
6. **Action 4:** Archive the original file in SharePoint

The result?  
That boring 1-hour Monday routine? It now takes **zero minutes**.

And the best part: **It runs while I sleep.**

## 🧠 What I Learned Along the Way

- **Start simple.** Use templates before building from scratch.
- **Test often.** The first flow won’t be perfect — debug as you go.
- **Think in triggers and actions.** Power Automate is like Lego for your workflows.
- **Connect with your ecosystem.** Outlook, Excel, SharePoint, Teams — they all work together seamlessly.

## 🚀 How It Changed My Workday

After that first win, I didn’t stop. I automated:

- Leave approvals using Microsoft Forms
- Recurring reminders in Teams
- Daily data refreshes in Power BI
- Slack-to-Outlook message forwarding

Power Automate didn’t just save time.  
It **freed up mental space** to focus on tasks that actually matter.
