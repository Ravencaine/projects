# Date.QuarterOfYear

Returns a number from 1 to 4 indicating which quarter of the year the date dateTime falls in. dateTime can be a date, datetime, or datetimezone value. Example Find which quarter of the year the date #date(2011, 12, 31) falls in. Usage Power Query M Date.QuarterOfYear(#date(2011, 12, 31)) Output 4 Last updated on 03/24/2026 --- PAGE 546 ---

Date.QuarterOfYear(dateTime as any) as nullable number
